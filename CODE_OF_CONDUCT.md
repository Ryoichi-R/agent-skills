# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Assume good intent while addressing technical and safety concerns directly.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, sexualized conduct, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository discussions, contributions, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

For conduct reports or general contact, use https://github.com/Ryoichi-R/agent-skills/issues. Security vulnerabilities must follow SECURITY.md.
